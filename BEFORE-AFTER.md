## 🎯 Key Transformations

### Aesthetic Shift
- **FROM:** Dark, tech, SaaS, neon glow
- **TO:** Light, editorial, warm, considered calm
- **Reference:** Aesop, Frama, not tech startup

### Typography Hierarchy
- **FROM:** Ornate serif + geometric sans
- **TO:** Variable serif display + classic editorial + technical mono

### Color Temperature
- **FROM:** Cool (cyan, blue, dark navy)
- **TO:** Warm (clay, ochre, cream, bone)

### Interaction Language
- **FROM:** Glowing, animated, tech-forward
- **TO:** Precise, deliberate, understated

### Voice & Tone
- **FROM:** Marketing hype, superlatives
- **TO:** Researcher peer, exact, factual
